## Recording notes

- Keep the browser at 1280 × 720 and use the seeded `Winning scenario` state on the Policy Lab.
- When showing a changed slider, return to **Winning scenario** before the final screen.
- Use `contracts/fixtures/synthetic/admissions-valid.csv`, whose synthetic institution, campus and course match the local seeded approval. Do not show personal or real institution data.
- Pause for at least two seconds after every major state change so captions and results are readable.
- Keep the role selector visible when switching roles so judges understand that access depends on the current role.
- Say “synthetic adapter” or “local simulation” wherever an external service is shown. Do not claim an official connection or a real deployment.
- If the live service fails on the day, play the recorded walkthrough and use the README's local restart steps.
- The committed video is silent and captioned. Read this script as the voice-over or use it to present the same sequence live.
