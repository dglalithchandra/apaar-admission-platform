# Pilot incident and recovery

Declare severity, affected cohort/capabilities, synthetic owner, evidence references and automatic or manual stop action. Declaration and stopped execution version are one transaction.

For an identity-provider outage, retain identity work as `PENDING`/`RETRYABLE`; never convert an outage into an invalid APAAR result. Preserve durable checkpoints and accepted admissions. Record the timeline without student or institution identifiers in public metrics.

Resume requires recovery checks, cleared stop controls and an independent checker. Record recovery evidence and post-incident review append-only. If retry limits are exhausted, dead-letter the work for controlled casework rather than silently discarding it.
